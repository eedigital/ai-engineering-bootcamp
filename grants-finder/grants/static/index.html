Matching grants

Your matches will appear here, best first. For each grant you'll see:

  1. whether you're eligible, checked rule by rule,
  2. the grant's own words for each rule,
  3. what to find out before applying,
  4. a first draft of your application, on request.

Grant data comes live from the Grants.gov API. This product uses the Grants.gov API but is not endorsed or certified by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. Matches and drafts are written by AI and can be wrong: always read the full notice on Grants.gov before applying. See the code.
